Why Do Salons Miss More Calls During the Holiday Season?

Salons miss more calls in November and December precisely because they're busiest — not in spite of it. When every stylist is booked back-to-back and the front desk is managing a full floor, the phone becomes the lowest priority. The person calling to book a holiday party blowout doesn't get answered. They call the salon down the street.

Three things converge to create this problem in December specifically:

  • Call volume spikes, but staff doesn't. Holiday bookings bring a surge of new callers — people who want their hair done for a company party, a family gathering, or New Year's Eve. This is incremental demand on top of your regular client base, and it hits the phone hard.
  • Gift card inquiries flood in after hours. A large portion of holiday gift card purchases happen in the evening, when people are home shopping and planning gifts. If your salon closes at 7pm and doesn't answer calls until 9am, you're invisible to the most motivated buyers during their peak buying window.
  • Last-minute cancellations and rebooking calls happen at the worst possible time. When someone cancels a holiday appointment, they often call the same day — meaning your front desk gets the call mid-service, during their busiest moment, and either misses it or handles it poorly.

The result: your most profitable season is also your highest call-miss season. That's the specific problem this post is about.
