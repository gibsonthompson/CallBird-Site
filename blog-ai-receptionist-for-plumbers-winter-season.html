Why Are Winter Plumbing Calls Different From Every Other Season?

Winter plumbing calls are worth more, arrive at worse hours, and carry higher urgency than any other season — which makes missing them more expensive per call than any other time of year.

In summer, a missed call might be someone asking about a slow drain or wanting a water heater quote. They'll leave a voicemail, or call back tomorrow. In winter, the calls that come in after 9pm are almost always emergencies: burst pipes, frozen supply lines, failed water heaters, sump pump failures during a thaw. These are not "I'll try again in the morning" situations. Homeowners in crisis call the first plumber who answers — and if that's not you, it's your competitor.

The rate structure matters too. Emergency winter calls — especially after-hours, weekend, or holiday calls — typically command premium pricing. A job that would be a $300 repair at 2pm Tuesday becomes a $600-$900 job at midnight on Saturday. Missing one of those calls doesn't just cost you a customer. It costs you your highest-margin job of the week.
