Why Plumbers Miss More Calls Than Almost Any Other Trade

Plumbers miss calls for a concrete reason that no other fix addresses: the job physically prevents answering. You're under a sink with both hands on a wrench. You're in a crawl space with your head two feet from a corroded shutoff valve. You're on a ladder snaking a drain. These aren't situations where you can grab your phone — and a callback 45 minutes later, after a homeowner with a soaked floor has already called three other numbers, accomplishes nothing.

There are two categories of calls plumbers miss, and they have very different costs:

  • Routine service calls — leaky faucets, slow drains, toilet repairs, water heater checks. These happen during business hours. Value: $150–$350 typically. The caller will often leave a voicemail or try again later.
  • Emergency calls — burst pipes, sewage backups, no hot water in January, flooding. These happen at night, on weekends, and on holidays. Value: $450–$1,000+, often with emergency rate premiums. The caller will not leave a voicemail. They need someone on the phone right now.

Here's the problem with how most plumbers think about missed calls: they focus on the first category. But the second category — emergency calls when you're either asleep, off the clock, or unavailable to your phone — represents the highest revenue per call your business sees. And those are the calls with zero infrastructure behind them.

Most of your competitors have the same gap. The plumber who fills it wins every after-hours emergency in your market.

Step 2: Configure your plumbing-specific details. Step 5: Forward your calls. Set conditional call forwarding from your business line so when you're unavailable, calls route to CallBird automatically. Takes about 60 seconds through your carrier settings.
