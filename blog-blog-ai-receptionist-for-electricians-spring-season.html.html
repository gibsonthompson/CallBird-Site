Why Do Electricians Miss More Calls in Spring Than Any Other Season?

Spring creates a specific double bind that no other season replicates: your most labor-intensive jobs and your highest call volume happen at the same time.

Think about what fills a residential electrician's spring schedule. Panel upgrades ahead of AC season. EV charger installs as the weather improves. Outdoor outlet and lighting work that homeowners put off all winter. Generator hookups after the first spring storm runs through the area. These aren't quick service calls — they're four-to-eight-hour jobs that require sustained focus, two-hand involvement, and zero ability to answer your phone mid-task.

At the same time, spring brings the year's first storm season. A single significant storm — downed lines, tripped breakers, partial power loss across a neighborhood — generates a flood of calls in a two-hour window. Homeowners in that window are not comparison shopping. They want the first electrician who picks up.

The result: spring is the season when electricians are simultaneously the most in-demand and the least reachable. That gap is where revenue disappears.
